What was read

This group vs the published consensus

Every position where both the group and the published consensus define an ice thickness. The difference is group − published: positive means the group picked deeper than what was published.

Per contributor (hidden by default — click to expand)

Each contributor's deviation from the group's own consensus and from the published consensus, averaged across the radargrams they picked on.

How many contributors are enough?

A random subgroup of k contributors is drawn many times and compared with the published consensus. The closer the curve is to 100%, the fewer contributors are needed.

Steepness vs disagreement

The consensus' along-track thickness slope, as an angle in degrees, against how spread out the contributors are there. Spread is shown relative to each radargram's typical value, so 1.0× is normal; the line is the binned median and the band the 25–75% range. Hover for the position count behind each point.

The published reference is the 0.49 order statistic over the pooled bed picks of the full ridal interpretation set, accessed from the published data at doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17882299. Consensus engine and reference builder are validated against the study's own output; see README.md.
